## App Description

### App Store Description (4000 character limit)

**Know Your Sugar. Choose Better.**

SugarFlag gives you instant, AI-powered analysis of added sugars and sweeteners in any food product. Just scan a barcode or snap a photo of the nutrition label to get a clear 0-100 score with plain-English explanations.

**FEATURES**

📸 **Instant Scanning**
• Barcode (UPC) scanning for instant product lookup
• Photo scanning with AI-powered OCR for any nutrition label
• Encrypted photo processing for your privacy

🎯 **Smart Scoring**
• Clear 0-100 scores based on added sugars and sweetener quality
• Plain-English explanations, not complex nutrition jargon
• Detailed sweetener breakdown with health impact

🛒 **Personalized Recommendations**
• Weekly cart suggestions with lower-sugar alternatives
• Tailored to your dietary preferences and restrictions
• Discover healthier swaps for your favorite products

📊 **Track Your Progress**
• Complete scan history with scores and insights
• See your sugar consumption patterns over time
• Export your data anytime (GDPR compliant)

🔒 **Privacy First**
• Photos encrypted on-device before transmission (AES-GCM-256)
• Passkey authentication (WebAuthn) - no passwords to remember
• Your data, your control - delete anytime

**WHY SUGARFLAG?**

The average American consumes 77 grams of added sugar daily - nearly 3x the recommended limit. Hidden sugars lurk in "healthy" foods like yogurt, granola, and whole wheat bread. Marketing labels like "natural" and "organic" don't mean low-sugar.

SugarFlag cuts through the confusion. Our AI analyzes nutrition labels and ingredients to identify added sugars, natural sugars, and all types of sweeteners - from high fructose corn syrup to stevia. You get instant clarity on what you're really eating.

## Common Rejection Reasons to Avoid

1. **Misleading health claims:** Focus on information, not medical advice
2. **Incomplete features:** All advertised features must work
3. **Privacy policy missing:** Must be accessible before account creation
4. **Broken links:** Test all URLs in metadata
5. **Subscription unclear:** Benefits must be clearly stated
6. **IAP not working:** Test sandbox purchases thoroughly
7. **Crashes:** Test extensively before submission
8. **UI issues:** Follow Human Interface Guidelines
